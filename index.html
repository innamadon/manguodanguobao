Hong Kong · Nov 12–14

Wed Nov 11 — fly out (just after midnight)

CX811 Boston → Hong Kong departs 12:30 AM — that means Logan late on Tue Nov 10 night. Lands Hong Kong Thu Nov 12, 5:00 AM. Leave home ≈9:00 PM Tue — the drive to Logan is ≈30 min at that hour; be at the airport by ≈9:45–10:00 PM (3 hrs ahead for an international departure; Cathay check-in closes 1 hr before).

Thu Nov 12 — arrive

Land at 5:00 AM — immigration + bags ≈45–60 min, then ≈40 min by taxi to TST (or Airport Express ≈25 min to Kowloon Station + a short cab), so you’re at the Mondrian (TST) by ≈6:45–7:00 AM. Drop bags at the hotel — check-in is from 3:00 PM, so take the morning slow (harbour-front breakfast in TST). Light first evening: Victoria Harbour at sunset from the TST promenade, Tamjai Samgor (tomato soup, spice 3–4) or room-service pace. If it’s dry: wander the Jordan night markets (Temple Street — street food is liveliest ~7–10 PM, ~10 min from the hotel); if it rains, skip it and keep the early night.

Fri Nov 13 — full day

Tai Hang citywalk in the morning (Bing Kee Cha Dong, MUSE cafe, Tomo bar), Sister Wah brisket in Tin Hau for lunch. Afternoon: fragrance run — Archi, Llems, One Day (the rice scent) — and Twemco clocks. Egg-tart crawl as snacks dictate (Hashtag B was the pick). Evening: rain due (70%) — keep it flexible, bars over the night markets if it sets in: Jordan Night Markets or the after-dark bars (001, Savory Project).

Sat Nov 14 — to Shanghai

Slow breakfast, Hong Kong Museum of Art if it fits (it’s by the hotel), then CX360 to Shanghai, 1:35–4:15 PM — leave the Mondrian ≈10:45 AM (taxi ≈35 min to HKG, or the Airport Express from Kowloon Station) to be at the airport ≈2 hrs ahead. Benlai has a Hong Kong store at Langham Place if you want it early — otherwise the Guangzhou flagship below is the better visit.
